# Visual credits

The cat mesh, the fur, the eyes, and the whiskers are original work
for this game. There is no downloaded model.

- `tools/build_cat.py` — the body. Built in Blender 4.2 from
  cross-sections (skull, muzzle, chest, waist, haunch, digitigrade
  legs, toes, ears, tapered tail), fused with a voxel remesh and
  quadriflow, then subdivided. The armature, the clips (idle, walk,
  trot, sprint, stalk, pounce, land, swipe, meow, sit, lounge), and
  the albedo, normal, and roughness maps are generated by that
  script. Nothing in it is traced from a third-party mesh.
- `cat_kit/meshes/cat.glb` — that mesh, three LODs, and the clips.
- `cat_kit/meshes/willie_albedo.png`, `aggie_albedo.png`,
  `cat_normal.png`, `cat_roughness.png` — maps from the same script.
  Willie's ginger is `#E8AF54`. Aggie's patches are her palette.
- `visual/fur.gdshader` — shell fur, written here, skinned to the
  same skeleton as the body.
- `visual/cat_fidelity.gd` — shells, cornea, pupil, catchlight, whiskers.
- Coat colours stay Daniel's ruled spec in `cat_kit/cat_species.json`.
- No CC-BY assets are bundled, so there is no attribution line to add.
